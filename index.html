Controls (1 Player):

📱 Touch/drag on the board, tap the ▲ ▼ buttons below it, or 🖱️ move mouse — controls left paddle

⌨️ Arrow Up/Down or W/S also move the left paddle

🤖 Computer controls right paddle

Controls (2 Player):

Player 1 (left, yellow): W / S keys, or the left ▲ ▼ buttons

Player 2 (right, pink): Arrow Up / Arrow Down keys, or the right ▲ ▼ buttons

General:

⏸️ Press Space or the Pause button to pause

⛶ Press F or the Full Screen button for a bigger board — great for mobile, rotate to landscape for the best view

⚡ Power-ups float onto the field during rallies — hit them with your paddle for a bonus (paddle grow, opponent shrink, or ball slow-down)

🔥 Long rallies build a combo streak shown above the board

📳 On supported phones, scoring and power-ups give a short vibration

Score points to level up — the game gets faster and harder!

First to 11 points wins!
